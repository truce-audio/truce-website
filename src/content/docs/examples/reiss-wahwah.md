# Reiss Wah-Wah

<img src="/screenshots/reiss-mcpherson/wahwah.png" class="screenshot-hero" width="415" height="210" alt="Reiss Wah-Wah">

Resonant band-pass filter swept by one of three sources: manual
position, an LFO, or an envelope-follower on the input.

Source: [`reiss-mcpherson-wahwah/`](https://github.com/truce-audio/reiss-mcpherson-effects/tree/main/plugins/reiss-mcpherson-wahwah).

## What it demonstrates

- A resonant band-pass biquad with a sweepable center frequency
- Three modulation sources selected by `EnumParam`
- An envelope-follower with attack / release
