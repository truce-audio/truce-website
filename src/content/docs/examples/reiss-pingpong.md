# Reiss Ping-Pong

<img src="/screenshots/reiss-mcpherson/pingpong.png" class="screenshot-hero" width="277" height="113" alt="Reiss Ping-Pong">

Cross-channel feedback routing on top of a stereo delay - left
output feeds the right channel's delay and vice versa, producing
the bouncing stereo image.

Source: [`reiss-mcpherson-pingpong/`](https://github.com/truce-audio/reiss-mcpherson-effects/tree/main/plugins/reiss-mcpherson-pingpong).

## What it demonstrates

- Per-channel delay lines with cross-channel feedback
- A single time + feedback control driving both sides
